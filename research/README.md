## 08-provenance — the proof, not the claim

Answers the question *on what basis did you collect this?* with bytes on disk
rather than a sentence. For each source: the site's own `robots.txt` as served,
the response headers of the exact URLs we read (with `X-Robots-Tag` and
`Content-Signal` pulled out by name), the licence page as served, and a SHA-256
of each — under `evidence/legal/<SID>/<UTC>/`, never edited, never deleted.

- **[08-provenance/README.md](08-provenance/README.md)** — the contract: evidence before collector, an unknown is never a permission, we identify honestly.
- **[08-provenance/INDEX.md](08-provenance/INDEX.md)** — **generated** by `tools/build_provenance_index.py`; permitted, refused, incomplete, and the undocumented work queue. Never edit by hand.
- **[08-provenance/EDGE_CASES.md](08-provenance/EDGE_CASES.md)** — where the law is unsettled: text and data mining with no Serbian rule, the absence of a licence, repeated extraction adding up to a substantial one, the door we stopped at, archives and live opt-outs, the word "measurement" for sound, and the one nobody regulates — what obligations lawful aggregation acquires that its parts did not. Each entry names what is soft, what we do anyway, and the question we would put.
- **[08-provenance/CORRECTIONS.md](08-provenance/CORRECTIONS.md)** — every time this system stated something false, and what it said. Append-only.
