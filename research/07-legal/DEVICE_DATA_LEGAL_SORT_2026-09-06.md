# Legal sorting of the device and data discovery wave

This is the operational classification of eight source records reviewed in the [device discovery wave](../02-senses/DEVICE_DATA_DISCOVERY_2026-09-06.md). It applies the existing [collection legal frame](COLLECTION_LEGAL_FRAME.md) and [edge-case decisions](../08-provenance/EDGE_CASES.md), especially E-003 (public-enterprise facts) and E-004 (cumulative extraction). It does not revalidate all legal claims in those earlier documents or clear the other 178 registry records.

Access signals, reuse basis, product scope, evidence retention, raw redistribution and repeated collection are separate questions. A successful HTTP request and a permissive robots result answer neither the licensing question nor the cumulative-extraction question. The generated [provenance index](../08-provenance/INDEX.md) now renders access checks and these legal categories separately; its access-decision algorithm is unchanged.

## Licence evidence and attribution

**RATEL.** The [Serbian portal terms](https://data.gov.rs/sr/terms/) allow copying, distribution and adaptation, including metadata, for commercial and noncommercial use subject to the stated conditions. Reuse must identify the authority, source URL and retrieval date and disclose changes. The [continuous-measurement catalogue](https://data.gov.rs/sr/datasets/rezultati-kontinualnog-merenja-nivoa-elektrichnog-polja-na-lokatsijama-od-interesa/?resources=all) explicitly labels the Serbian Open Data Licence and links `/getOpenData/21/json`. The [drive-measurement catalogue](https://data.gov.rs/sr/datasets/emf-drajv-merenja/?resources=all) links `/open-data-2-2.json` as Belgrade November 2024. Its displayed older label is “Javni podaci”; the portal's default grant supplies the reuse basis. Its inconsistent Belgrade CSV link is not silently treated as the same resource.

The three pages are stored with hashes in the S158 licence capture manifest (private working record). RATEL attribution and transformations (private working record) accompany the portable gzip and derived checks. Today's licence capture does not repair the historical wrong-host collection gate by implication; [C-007](../08-provenance/CORRECTIONS.md) preserves that error.

**ACTRIS.** The [approved licensing policy](https://actris.eu/sites/default/files/inline-files/ACTRIS_ERIC_GA_approved_ACTRIS%20licensing.pdf) and [current data policy](https://data.actris.eu/data-policy) distinguish required CC BY 4.0 categories from recommendations for level 3 and legacy level 2. This distinction prevents a generic “all ACTRIS data are CC BY” claim. Both policy documents are stored in the S186 licence capture (private working record).

**AERONET.** The [publisher homepage](https://aeronet.gsfc.nasa.gov/) declares the database public domain; [measurement-use guidance](https://aeronet.gsfc.nasa.gov/data_usage.html) asks for contributor recognition and consultation. The declaration is stored in the S185 capture (private working record). Catalogue coverage and measurement reuse are kept distinct.

## Collection and release boundaries

This wave made bounded discovery captures and analysed already retained data. It established no new continuous collector. For sources lacking a covering grant, E-004 requires a preregistered minimum field set, cadence, horizon and shared request/byte budget before any repeated extraction is considered. That operational discipline does not resolve the legal uncertainty by itself. An opt-out or unresolved access verdict still stops collection regardless of a reuse category.

E-003 evidence retention is a project decision with an explicitly recorded unresolved legal question, not a publisher licence. Unlicensed HTML/PDF originals stay local review evidence; they are not approved as public products by this report or a local repository checkpoint. Before a public release, select artifacts by their actual reuse scope, preserve attribution, and check current conditions. No EU TDM exception is assumed as a Serbian permission.

The register stores eight `legal_review` objects with category, basis, scope, retention, redistribution, repeat-collection, attribution and evidence fields. The matrix covers this wave only. Exact-route capture enforcement and schema validation remain engineering follow-ups named in C-007; the existing collector was not changed here. An additive metadata correction (private working record) records that the attribution artifact's creation field reused the licence-capture time; source retrieval dates and hashes are unaffected.
