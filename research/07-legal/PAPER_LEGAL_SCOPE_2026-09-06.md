# Legal and institutional questions for the pre-paper

The relevant question is how legal conditions shape the evidence available for urban interpretation. Access, collection, retention, repeated extraction, analysis and redistribution are separate actions. This audit extends the [collection frame](COLLECTION_LEGAL_FRAME.md) and [current-product review](CURRENT_PRODUCTS_LEGAL_2026-09-06.md); it does not clear all 189 sources or implement new collection.

## Two updates to the earlier framing

**Database extraction:** the Serbian copyright text consolidated through 66/2019 in [WIPO Lex](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/19376) resolves the previously truncated Article 138 reading. It covers systematic extraction/reuse of insubstantial parts where normal exploitation or legitimate interests are harmed, alongside substantial-part rights. Article 137 concerns qualifying investment. Article 140b also protects lawful users' extraction/reuse of insubstantial parts, subject to its normal-exploitation and rights qualifications. These provisions need to be read together. A succession of small requests is therefore not automatically outside database-rights analysis. The [Ministry's copyright-law page](https://privreda.gov.rs/lat/dokumenta/propisi/zakoni/zakon-o-autorskom-i-srodnim-pravima) remains a primary route for checking the text. No Serbian text-and-data-mining exception is relied on in this project; this bounded review does not assert an exhaustive absence across all current legislation.

**Recent eGovernment amendment:** Parliament's [adopted-laws index](https://www.parlament.gov.rs/akti/doneti-zakoni/donetizakoni.01.1033.html) lists adoption on 31 August 2026 and Gazette 80/26. The [adopted text](https://www.parlament.gov.rs/upload/archive/files/lat/pdf/zakoni/14_saziv/1844-26%20-%20Lat..pdf) addresses dynamic/research/high-value data, reuse exceptions, attribution and open formats. Its Article 27a provides API publication conditions for dynamic data, including disproportionate-burden qualifications. Article 27b addresses high-value datasets. Transition provisions set **1 January 2030** deadlines for the specified smart-city platform and Article 27b reuse duties, and **1 March 2027** for specified bylaws. Commencement is the eighth day after Gazette publication. Exact effective date is not established here from an independently inspected official Gazette issue. Adoption, commencement, transitional deadlines and actual implementation must remain distinct; this is not evidence of an operational Belgrade platform or immediate access to every dataset.

## Sorting dimensions for each product

| Dimension | What to record | Why it matters for planning research |
|---|---|---|
| Technical access | Exact route, date, response/access state, relevant machine restrictions | An accessible page may still lack reusable files |
| Rights holder and grant | Actual resource/version, licence, attribution, any upstream material | Metadata, article and measurement-file rights can differ |
| Collection and reuse | Allowed purpose, extent, recurrence and redistribution scope | A local receipt is not permission for a continuing public mirror |
| Personal data | Whether records concern identifiable people; applicable purpose and safeguards | Combining seemingly coarse data may create new identification risks |
| Sector conditions | Hydromet, geospatial and other source-specific provisions | General openness arguments may leave material qualifications |
| Legal time | Text version, adoption/effective/application dates and review date | A newly adopted rule is not proof of current implementation |
| Institutional meaning | Publisher duty, procurement, installation and actual operating evidence | Administrative intent and operational capability require different proof |

## Comparative legal context and unresolved research

The [EU copyright directive 2019/790](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/790/oj/eng), Articles 3–4, supplies a comparator for lawful-access mining, eligible research activity and rights reservations. It is not automatic authorization for a Serbian project. The [Serbian Commissioner](https://poverenik.rs/zastita-podataka-o-licnosti/) provides the personal-data-law route; this wave checked the official page but did not obtain a newly readable complete law PDF. Existing source-specific privacy analysis therefore remains the record to inspect before any new personal-data use.

Discussion should retain three open items: verify the new law's exact Gazette publication/effective date before relying on it operationally; assess resource-level reuse before a new dataset analysis; and distinguish legal permissibility from representational fairness and public participation. Legal openness alone cannot establish equitable planning outcomes.
