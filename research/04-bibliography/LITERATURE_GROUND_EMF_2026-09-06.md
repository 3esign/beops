## G1 — BEOG daily GNSS coordinates

Tucikešić, Sanja; Blagojević, Dragan. (2019). **Modelling of the time-series of GNSS coordinates and their interaction with average magnitude earthquakes.** *Geodetski vestnik, 63*(4), 525–540. DOI: **10.15292/geodetski-vestnik.2019.04.525-540**.

Primary: [publisher record](https://www.geodetski-vestnik.com/en/clanek/10.15292/geodetski-vestnik.2019.04.525-540); [publisher PDF](https://www.geodetski-vestnik.com/63/4/gv63-4_tucikesic.pdf). Read level: targeted full text, including data and time-series modelling.

The study processes actual permanent-station GNSS coordinate observations from **BEOG, Belgrade**, with Sarajevo's SRJV as a comparison. BEOG observations span **August 2014–March 2019**, using daily IGS08 solutions. Receiver make and precise BEOG street address were not established. The publication date does not establish the latest operational reading.

Data provenance points to Nevada Geodetic Laboratory's [GlobalStationList](http://geodesy.unr.edu/NGLStationPages/GlobalStationList/). Opening that general route failed here; an exact current BEOG product and its licence remain unverified. No series was collected. Article licence version and separate NGL product rights were not confirmed.

**BEOPS consequence:** preserve coordinate reference frame, seasonal modelling and noise alongside apparent displacement. The earthquake analysis does not establish earthquake prediction. This is a historical local dataset connection with a concrete institutional data provider, not a verified live feed.

## G2 — permanent GNSS monitoring at Umka

Samardžić-Petrović, Mileva; Popović, Jovan; Đurić, Uroš; Abolmasov, Biljana; Pejić, Marko; Marjanović, Miloš. (2020). **Permanent GNSS monitoring of landslide Umka.** *STEPGRAD, 14*, 91–98. DOI: **10.7251/STP2014091S**.

Primary: [publisher, published 10 June 2020](https://doisrpska.nub.rs/index.php/STPG/article/view/6803); [University of Belgrade institutional full text](https://dr.rgf.bg.ac.rs/files/original/3eb29255759c7998527e8ef6633bde565dc422b9.pdf). Read level: targeted full text.

Actual displacement monitoring at **Umka, Čukarica, on the Sava's right bank** began in March 2010. A building-mounted GNSS receiver, three AGROS reference stations and Leica Spider/GeoMoS form the system; receiver model was not confirmed. Acquisition is **30 seconds**. The 62 additional survey points are passive marks, not 62 automatic sensors.

Displayed series cover 2010–December 2013 and September 2014–December 2018; receiver relocation occurred in December 2013 and gaps exist. Survey epochs include March/November 2018 and early April 2019.

The institutional paper is publicly accessible, but no public Umka GNSS/GeoMoS endpoint or separately licensed raw series was found. Current operation is unverified.

**BEOPS consequence:** represent instrument relocation and outages in observation history. Historical high-frequency acquisition does not establish a current public service. G3 partly reuses this monitoring context.

## E1 — seven Belgrade sites and temporal RF variability

Koprivica, Mladen; Petrić, Majda; Popović, Milica; Milinković, Jelena; Nešković, Aleksandar. (2016). **Empirical Analysis of Electric Field Strength Long-Term Variability for GSM/DCS/UMTS Downlink Band.** *Telfor Journal, 8*(2), 87–92. DOI: **10.5937/telfor1602087K**.

Primary: [publisher DOI record](https://journal.telfor.rs/Published/Vol8No2/Vol8No2.aspx); [publisher full text](https://journal.telfor.rs/Published/Vol8No2/Vol8No2_A4.pdf); [ETF full author metadata](https://www.etf.bg.ac.rs/sr-lat/fakultet/zaposleni/mladen-koprivica-1305). Read level: targeted full text. Published **20 November 2016**; expands TELFOR 2014 rather than proving a second campaign.

Actual measurements used calibrated **Rohde & Schwarz FSH6 and TS-EMF isotropic probe**, 30 MHz–3 GHz, with White Tigress Baby software. Five indoor and two outdoor Belgrade sites were each observed for seven days, stationary, at **10-second RMS sampling**. Calendar dates and addresses were not established.

Section II, p. 88 states: “Center frequency - 2140MHz and Channel bandwidth - 60MHz (DCS band)”. The next item assigns 1830.1 MHz to UMTS, conflicting with the introductory band naming. Preserve this inconsistency; do not silently relabel data.

The PDF is public; an explicit reuse grant and raw series were not found. This is historical evidence.

**BEOPS consequence:** store averaging interval and time-of-day context alongside RF measurements.

## E4 — school substation magnetic-field mitigation

Grbić, Maja; Canova, Aldo; Giaccone, Luca; Pavlović, Aleksandar; Grasso, Sergio. (2025). **Mitigation of Low Frequency Magnetic Field Emitted by 10/0.4 kV Substation in the School.** *International Journal of Numerical Modelling: Electronic Networks, Devices and Fields*. DOI: **10.1002/jnm.70015**.

Primary: [Wiley abstract and metadata](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/jnm.70015). Read level: **publisher abstract and data-availability statement**, not full methods. First published **12 March 2025**.

Actual **50 Hz magnetic flux-density** measurements concern a classroom above a 10/0.4 kV substation in a **Belgrade primary school**. The accessible primary abstract did not establish the school's name, campaign dates or probe model.

Measurements compare conditions before and after replacing ceiling busbars with floor-mounted cables. Seven passive shielding options are numerically analysed; do not claim all were installed. The modelled 6.95 μT result at rated load is not itself a field reading.

The publisher says data are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request. That is neither a public download nor a redistribution licence; no contact was made. Article licence and current monitoring were not confirmed.

**BEOPS consequence:** magnetic flux density in μT and RF electric field in V/m are different observables requiring separate instruments, units and interpretation.
