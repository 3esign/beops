Kako ovo radiHow this works

Svakih pet minuta program na jednom računaru na univerzitetu pita izvore koji su to javno dozvolili (dozvola je sačuvana kao fajl: robots.txt, zaglavlja, licenca, sa hešom). Svaki odgovor dobija tri vremena: kad je izmereno (ako izvor to uopšte kaže), kad je objavljeno, kad je primljeno. Nedostajuća vrednost je null, nikad nula. Ništa se ne popunjava, ne zaglađuje, ne prognozira.

Every five minutes a program on one university computer asks the sources that publicly permit it (the permission is stored as bytes: robots.txt, headers, licence page, hashed). Every answer gets three times: when it was measured (if the source says at all), when it was published, when we received it. A missing value is null, never zero. Nothing is filled, smoothed or forecast.

Šta monolog sme da kažeWhat the monologue may say

Svaka rečenica ovde je napravljena iz zapisa o prijemu, ne iz modela: ko je govorio, koliko redova, za koji sat, kad je primljeno. Kad se organ (mali lokalni model) oglasi, njegova rečenica je označena isprekidanim krugom i rečju model, i nikad ne sadrži broj kojeg nema u zapisima. Kad model ne odgovara, monolog kaže da organ ćuti.

Every sentence here is built from reception records, not from a model: who spoke, how many rows, for which hour, when received. When an organ (a small local model) speaks, its sentence is marked with a dashed ring and the word model, and never contains a number that is not in the records. When the model does not answer, the monologue says the organ is silent.

Šta mapa sme da pokažeWhat the map may show

Tačka je instrument na svojoj stvarnoj koordinati (stanice SEPA, građanski senzori, parkirališta po linku operatera). Krug oko tačke nastaje samo kad je taj instrument pročitan u tom trenutku. Reke i izgrađeno tkivo dolaze iz Natural Earth-a 1:10 miliona (javno vlasništvo) — generalizovano, za orijentaciju, ne za merenje. Granica opština nema: svaki put do njih je odbijen i svako odbijanje je zabeleženo. Tihi šestougaonici su ljudi po H3 ćeliji (Kontur 2022, CC BY) — modelska procena, ne popis. Nema pojedinaca, nema kamera, nema ulica.

A dot is an instrument at its real coordinate (SEPA stations, citizen sensors, parking lots by the operator's link). A ring around a dot appears only when that instrument was read at that moment. Rivers and the built-up footprint come from Natural Earth 1:10 million (public domain) — generalised, for orientation, not for measurement. There are no municipality boundaries: every route to them was refused and every refusal is recorded. The quiet hexagons are people per H3 cell (Kontur 2022, CC BY) — a modelled estimate, not a census. No persons, no cameras, no streets.
