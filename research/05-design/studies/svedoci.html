BEOPS · SVEDOCI · dva pitanja na koja zapis može da odgovori o samom sebitwo questions the record can answer about itself

Oba dela ispod su aritmetika nad redovima koje ovaj zapis već ima, pod dozvolama koje je već imao. Nijedan izvor nije dodat, niko ništa nije pitan, i ništa se ovde ne prikuplja — ovo je ono što priznanice već kažu, pročitano nazad.

Both halves below are arithmetic over rows this record already holds, under permissions it already had. No source was added, nobody was asked for anything, and nothing here is collected — it is what the receipts already say, read back.

Koliko je bilo staro kad je stiglo do nasHow old it was when it reached us

Pokrivenost kaže da li grad objavljuje neki signal. Ovo kaže kada. Vodostaj star četiri sata je drugi instrument od onog starog četiri minuta, i nijedan izdavač ne kaže koji je njegov.

Coverage says whether a city publishes a signal. This says when. A river level four hours old is a different instrument from one four minutes old, and no publisher states which theirs is.

Ovo nije zakašnjenje izdavača. Pitamo u stalnom taktu, pa vrednost objavljena sekund pošto smo pitali čeka ceo interval dok je ne vidimo. Svaka starost ispod sadrži zakašnjenje izdavača plus do jednog našeg intervala, i ovaj zapis to dvoje ne može da razdvoji. Šrafirana traka je naš sopstveni interval, nacrtan u razmeri: gde je šira od stubića, nijedan deo te starosti ne može da se pripiše izdavaču. Nije ni ocena kvaliteta — časovni prosek ne može da bude svežiji od pola sata i nije zbog toga lošiji.

This is not the publisher's delay. We ask on a fixed cadence, so a value published one second after we asked waits a whole interval before we see it. Every age below contains the publisher's delay plus up to one interval of ours, and this record cannot separate them. The hatched band is our own interval, drawn to scale: where it is wider than the bar, none of that age can be attributed to the publisher at all. It is also not a quality score — an hourly mean cannot be fresher than half an hour and is not worse for it.

Da li dvojica od njih kažu istoWhether two of them say the same thing

Ova opservatorija nema nijedan sopstveni senzor, pa nikada ne može da tvrdi tačnost: svaka vrednost nasleđuje tuđu kalibraciju i tuđi sat. Ali gde dva izvora koja nikome zajedničkom ne odgovaraju opisuju isti parametar u istom satu, razlika među njima je činjenica koju ovaj zapis može da izračuna, a nijedan izdavač je ne objavljuje.

This observatory owns no sensor, so it can never state accuracy: every value inherits somebody else's calibration and somebody else's clock. But where two sources that answer to nobody in common describe the same parameter in the same hour, the difference between them is a fact this record can compute and neither publisher states.

Ovo nije mera greške i nije provera ikoga. Izvori stoje na različitim mestima — pista, padina, balkon — pa svaki razmak ispod sadrži i stvarnu razliku po gradu i eventualno neslaganje instrumenata, a ovaj zapis to dvoje ne može da razdvoji. Širok razmak nije dokaz da neko greši. On je dokaz da „temperatura u Beogradu" nije jedan broj — što je upravo ono što skriva tabla koja ispiše jedan broj. Ništa se ovde ne koristi da se bilo čija vrednost podesi, uskladi ili pretpostavi drugoj.

This is not an error bar and not a check of anyone. The sources stand in different places — a runway, a hillside, a balcony — so every gap below contains real variation across the city as well as any instrumental disagreement, and this record cannot separate the two. A wide gap is not evidence that somebody is wrong. It is evidence that “the temperature in Belgrade” is not a single number, which is what a dashboard printing one number hides. Nothing here is used to adjust, reconcile or prefer any source's value.
