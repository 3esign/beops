The state alphabet

This is what Belgrade told us, when it told us, and where it went quiet.

1 — the five marks

A stroke says where the value came from. A ground says how well we know its time. Nothing else changes. Colour is redundant: every mark differs in stroke and ground before hue is applied, so the set survives greyscale, colour blindness and a bad monitor.

2 — the ribbon: thirteen slots of one day

Time is the axis, because time is our actual problem. Three morning slots were missed and are drawn as missed — a hollow tick is a mark about nothing, not the absence of a mark. Two slots are real observations, each carrying an age band that fades away to the left because the source publishes no measurement time, so the band has no left edge to claim. Eight slots have not happened yet.

3 — the quorum bar: absence where there is no pixel to grey

A zone of forty sensors with three dead ones has no single pixel to grey out — the trick every good map interface relies on dies exactly at the resolution BEOPS chose. So we stop drawing the missing thing and draw how much of what should have spoken did speak. Counting, not estimating. Here the same bar is used over the 27 locations of one response, and over five hypothetical zone sources to show the three cell states: filled, hollow, hatched.

4 — the values, both real samples

Magnitude is length, never colour. Observed-but-untimed values carry the reduced-chroma signal; the delta column is structure, not signal, because a delta is something we computed, not something the city said.

Everything drawn here is from sample-20260905T133000Z.json and sample-20260905T143000Z.json — real HTTP 200 responses, 47 313 and 47 314 bytes, hashes e6e8d451b1dcee89… and 4fd153cd0c1c5611…. The three missed slots are real misses. Nothing on this page is filled in, interpolated, smoothed or invented, and no value on it is a forecast — the forecast glyph in section 1 is shown as a specimen of the alphabet, labelled as such, and is not attached to any BEOPS number.

This is a legibility study by one designer, not a comprehension test. Two marks here — the unbounded age band and the quorum bar — have no published research behind them, mine or anyone's. If the age band reads as a rendering error rather than as “we don't know when”, the invention has failed and that is the finding.
