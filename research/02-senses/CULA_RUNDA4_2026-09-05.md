# BEOPS — Round 4: live feeds, cameras, and the sound of the city

Status: current
Date: 2026-09-05
Author: claude-cowork

The question this round answered: **what can be collected continuously and lawfully that gives a
picture of Belgrade as it changes — including news and notices, and including cameras and sound?**

The legal half of the answer is in [`../07-legal/COLLECTION_LEGAL_FRAME.md`](../07-legal/COLLECTION_LEGAL_FRAME.md).
This file is what exists.

---

## 0. The shape of the answer, in five lines

1. **News and notices are the richest live layer, and they work today** — a dozen verified RSS/Atom
   feeds, several with proper timestamps and same-hour freshness.
2. **Four sources name ClaudeBot in `robots.txt` and are therefore off limits.** One of them,
   Beobuild, is the single best source on the physical change of the city. It gets a letter.
3. **Institutions are a desert.** `beograd.rs` has no feed. The city assembly publishes no
   machine-readable agenda. The city gazette is PDF only. **One of seventeen municipalities has RSS.**
4. **No camera over Belgrade may lawfully be harvested.** Two passes, every aggregator traced back to
   Windy, Naxi/AMSS or SkylineWebcams, all of which refuse. What is clean is satellite: Sentinel-2
   and hourly Meteosat, both openly licensed.
5. **The city's sound exists as an instrument and not as data.** Serbia's seismic network is
   registered open with a DOI — and its archive is seven months stale with nothing at all from the
   station in the city centre.

### Off limits, and why

**Named ClaudeBot opt-outs**, with Cloudflare `Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-train=no,
use=reference`: **Beobuild**, **Zoomer**, **Insajder**, **eKapija** — eKapija citing EU Directive
2019/790 explicitly. **Politika** disallows `*/rss` behind a ~800-agent blocklist and a 2-second
crawl delay. **Vreme** and **Nedeljnik** disallow `/feed/`.

A research pass proposed collecting Beobuild "with a collector not identified as Claude". **Refused,
and recorded as refused.** Circumventing a named opt-out is the first thing anyone would rightly hold
against an evidence-first project. Beobuild is the best source on the physical change of Belgrade
that exists — which is exactly why the correct move is a letter, on the same logic as the ten
traffic counters: a named owner, a named ask.

### The ownership fact that changes how four feeds are weighted

N1, Nova.rs, Danas and Radar were United Group's Adria News Network — long regarded as Serbia's main
independent outlets. **As of August 2026 they are being sold to Alpac Capital, with the European
Board for Media Services reviewing the deal for concentration.** This surfaced live inside the Nova
Ekonomija feed during the pass and was corroborated three ways. The editorial independence of those
four feeds is now an open question, not a settled assumption, and the registry says so.

Informer, Kurir and the Pink/Happy ecosystem are documented as closely aligned with government
messaging — usable for service content (traffic, weather, incidents), weak for anything political.
KRIK, BIRN, Cenzolovka and Mašina are donor-funded or investigative: low volume, high value.

### Institutions: the desert, named

**No feed found, site reachable, checked directly:** `beograd.rs` (vesti, najave and sitemap all
checked), **Skupština grada Beograda**, **Službeni list grada Beograda** (real, searchable, newest
issue 76/2026 of 24.08.2026 — **PDF only**), MUP saopštenja, GSP Beograd, Direkcija za građevinsko
zemljište (beoland.com — real dated *Oglasi*, no feed), URBEL, FoNet, Euronews Serbia, and **ten of
the seventeen municipalities**. Five more could not be reached at all.

**Two of these are the most valuable missing feeds in the city.** URBEL's *javni uvid* notices and
the Direkcija's *oglasi* are legally required announcements that **a specific piece of the city is
about to change**. That is a forecast, not a report — a different and more valuable class than
anything in the media table. Neither is machine-readable.

**And the cleanest corpus is also the least used**: the Službeni list is *official material of a body
exercising public function* and under Copyright Act Art. 6(2) is **not a copyright work at all**. It
can be collected and republished in full, without permission. It is published as PDF, which is a
solvable engineering problem, not a legal one.

## PART B — Cameras: the honest answer after two passes

**There is no camera imaging Belgrade whose own terms permit continuous harvesting.**

Every aggregator opened — kamerebeograd.rs, svezavideonadzor.rs, 011info.com, see.cam, meteoblue —
terminates at one of three origins, all of which refuse:

- **AMSS / Naxi Media Group**, 19 Belgrade locations (Slavija, Gazela, Most na Adi, Brankov,
  Autokomanda, Pančevački, Terazije tunnel, Bulevar Mihajla Pupina…): *"Strogo je zabranjeno svako
  korišćenje kamera na bilo kojoj drugoj Web lokaciji ili računarskoj mreži bez pisanog odobrenja."*
- **Windy**: *"Redistribution of any part of the Services… is forbidden"*; *"continuous scanning of a
  significant amount of available Webcams"* is a **material breach**. Their staff has already refused
  API access to a competing weather service — BEOPS would read as exactly that.
- **SkylineWebcams (VisioRay)**: content *"cannot be used, modified, adapted, reformatted… without
  prior written authorization"*. Deriving a scalar is arguably modification.

The "Ada bridge" camera on 011info resolves to `stream.uzivobeograd.rs` → **NAXI KAMERE**. Zlatibor
publishes exactly the stable updating `.jpg` we hoped to find, under *"Zabranjeno je preuzimanje
sadržaja bez dozvole"*.

**The city's own ~660 cameras are MUP-operated and not public.** RHMZ, the airport, Plovput, the
rowing clubs and the marinas have no public camera. **No all-sky camera exists over Belgrade**, and
there is **no GMN, FRIPON or AllSky7 station anywhere in Serbia** — Croatia 43, Bulgaria 17, Hungary
3, Romania 3, Serbia zero. Vidojevica has an all-sky camera in hardware and publishes nothing.

### What is clean

- **Copernicus Sentinel-2** — *"free, full and open access"* for *"reproduction, distribution,
  communication, adaptation, modification"*, attribution only. 10/20/60 m, ~5-day revisit.
- **EUMETSAT Meteosat Core Data, hourly — CC-BY-4.0**, *"Users may redistribute all Core Data and
  Products."* Continuous, forever, ~1–3 km. The 5-minute Rapid Scan tier is paid (€4 000–8 000/yr)
  and Serbia cannot claim the national-met-service exemption.

### Two silences worth a letter

Silence is not permission, but it is a different posture from refusal. **Skijališta Srbije**
(Kopaonik) publishes fourteen fixed views with **no terms of use anywhere on the page**. And the
border cameras at `kamere.amss.org.rs` carry a footer crediting **"Ауто-мото савез Србије и
Министарство унутрашњих послова"** — **the Ministry of the Interior is a co-owner**, which makes it
the best-positioned recipient of a short written request of anything found in either pass.

### The instrument to build instead

A **zenith-pointed all-sky camera on a university roof**: Raspberry Pi + a ZWO ASI224MC-class sensor
+ fisheye, open-source `allsky` software, **$200–400**. It sees only sky, so it raises no
personal-data question at all — under EDPB Guidelines 3/2019 §8, *"the Regulation does not apply to
processing of data that has no reference to a person"*. University-owned, so the entire terms-of-service
layer disappears. And the method for cloud cover from such an image is published and CPU-cheap:
ELIFAN, red/blue ratio thresholding against a clear-sky reference, ~97–98% agreement outside sunrise
and sunset (doi:10.5194/amt-12-5519-2019).

Two published, transferable methods for street-level cameras exist if permission is ever granted:
**wet pavement**, 81.5% accuracy on completely unseen camera sites (arXiv:2510.06440), and **snow
cover** from *"publicly available webcam images"* (doi:10.5194/tc-14-1409-2020). **Crowd density** has
a method that never localises individuals (Chan, Liang & Vasconcelos, CVPR 2008) — but its *input* is
still an ordinary frame, so the protection is architectural, not optical. No published method
guarantees end-to-end non-identifiability. That would be our own systems claim, and we should not
make it.

## PART C — Sound

### There is no public sound of Belgrade

No continuous audio feed of any Belgrade public space was verified. Radio streams are content, not
city sound, and are copyrighted. No WebSDR or KiwiSDR receiver exists in Serbia — and **sdr.hu, the
canonical directory, has permanently shut down**. No Sensor.Community noise node, NoiseCapture,
NoiseTube or Hush City presence was confirmed for Belgrade. No AudioMoth deployment or open
bioacoustic dataset covers the Balkans.

### The gap is the opportunity, and it is documented

**Zakon o zaštiti od buke u životnoj sredini** (96/2021) Art. 21/22 require strategic noise maps and
action plans for agglomerations, and **Art. 24 requires *kontinuirano praćenje stanja buke*** —
continuous monitoring of the noise situation.

Belgrade's strategic noise map was **adopted 31 October 2025**, sixteen months after the law's own
deadline. And an environmental organisation states on record: **"Ne postoji monitoring buke koji
traje 365 dana u godini"** — measurement happens twice a year, spring and autumn. The city's own six
acoustic zones with limits from 50/40 to 65/55 dB day/night were set by City Assembly decision in
January 2022, and **nothing continuously checks whether they are met.**

**And Belgrade has lost its calibration event.** As of 2022, **49 of 118 warning sirens were
non-functional**, the last audible sounding was around the year 2000, and the city was replacing the
monthly audible test with a silent electronic check. A city of 1.7 million has no audible public
warning system. That is a finding in its own right.

### The node, costed

- **Hardware**: ICS-43434 I2S MEMS microphone (factory-calibrated, ±1 dB per datasheet) + ESP32 +
  weatherproof housing, **$20–35**.
- **Precedent, corrected**: SONYC is **not** level-only — its own paper states the nodes upload
  encrypted 10-second audio snippets. Its privacy model is encryption and access control, not
  non-capture. **The design that matches our rule is Sensor.Community's DNMS**, which has no audio
  path in the architecture at all: *"the individual sound pressure level values are integrated over
  time."*
- **Calibration is the whole difference**: a peer-reviewed $81 build reached **2.6 dB maximum error**
  across octave bands and R²=0.9723 against an urban reference (Mydlarz et al.). A naive
  ESP32+INMP441 build can be **off by ~10 dB**. Microphone choice and a 94 dB / 1 kHz reference tone
  are what separate a measurement from a number.
- **On-device classification exists**: **SONYC-L³, 1.17 MB, ~107 mW**, macro-AUPRC 0.579 — on a
  microcontroller. DCASE low-complexity acoustic scene classification runs at 128 kB / 30 MMACs for
  61.8% across ten venue types.
- **Publishes**: LAeq / L10 / L90 per broad zone per hour, tagged with source, instrument,
  calibration date and phenomenon time. **Refuses**: any audio, any sub-zone resolution, and any
  framing as an official measurement.

### The law, in three lines

- **KZ Art. 143** penalises unauthorised listening to and recording of a *razgovor* — a
  **conversation**. A device that computes a level and never writes an audio sample is not that.
- **ZZPL Art. 4**: personal data is data relating to an identified or identifiable natural person. A
  broad-zone sound level with no audio is not.
- **Noise law Art. 25 reserves official noise *measurement* to accredited bodies.** Our figures are
  **research observations** and must be labelled so on every screen. This is a real constraint and it
  is respected, not worked around.

### The instrument we already have, and the reason we do not have it

Serbia's seismic network is registered internationally as **FDSN network SJ**, carries **DOI
10.7914/SN/SJ**, and its metadata declares **`restrictedStatus="open"`** on the network and on both
relevant stations. EIDA routing names GEOFON as the data node. **AVAS** on Avala has **HHZ at 100 Hz**
— which covers the entire 1–20 Hz band where anthropogenic "cultural noise" lives; **BEO** in central
Belgrade has BHZ at 20 Hz, which reaches only the lower half of it.

This matters because seismic cultural noise **is** the sound of the city, and that is published
science: Lecocq et al., *Science* 369(6509):1338–1343, doi:10.1126/science.abd2438, measured the
global quieting of high-frequency seismic noise during lockdowns — the longest and most prominent
anthropogenic seismic noise reduction ever observed. Traffic, trams, construction, crowds.

**And then we queried the archive.** The availability extent, fetched directly:

| Channel | Rate | Coverage | Spans | Restriction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AVAS HHE/HHN/HHZ | 100 Hz | 2024-12-05 → **2026-01-28** | 16 | `OPEN` |
| AVAS EHE/EHN/EHZ | 100 Hz | 2026-01-28 → **2026-02-03** | 2 | `None` |
| AVAS SHE/SHN/SHZ | 20 Hz | 2026-01-28 → 2026-02-03 | 10 | `None` |
| **BEO BHE/BHN/BHZ** | 20 Hz | **no availability rows returned at all** | — | — |

`dataselect` returns **204 No Content** even inside the declared window.

**So the last seismic record of Belgrade available to Europe is seven months old, and the station in
the centre of the city delivers nothing.** The network is declared open and the archive is
effectively empty.

This is the fifth instance today of one pattern: **AGROS** has 29 GNSS stations behind a login-gated
brochure; **RATEL** has a real spectrum register with no export; **Plovput** has 18 RIS base stations
and calls the system open with no technical door; **BVK** has a laboratory and a page with no
numbers; and the **seismological institute** has a network registered as open with an empty archive.
*The instrument exists, the law permits, and the data does not flow.*

Which makes this the easiest request this project will ever write, because it asks for nothing
exceptional — only for what the institute has already declared: *your network is registered with the
FDSN as open, with a DOI; would you submit continuous AVAS and BEO waveforms to EIDA?* One university
colleague asking another, for a paper.

## Honest verdict

Done: seventeen live feeds opened and read with their newest item timestamped at fetch; seven named
opt-outs recorded with their clauses; the institutional feed desert mapped by name; two camera passes
concluding with two openly licensed satellite sources and two silences worth a letter; the sound
question answered through the noise law, the criminal code and the seismic archive; and the seismic
availability queried directly rather than taken on report.

Not done, and not claimed: nothing was collected — no feed is being polled, no camera fetched, no
waveform downloaded beyond a metadata and availability query. Nothing was sent to anyone. No
collector was built. Blic, NIN and RTS could not be reached at all and are unresolved rather than
absent. The Google News and Wikipedia routes failed at the tool level and carry no verified numbers.
Whether SEPA's and the city institute's air networks share physical cabinets is still unresolved, and
so is whether Serbian copyright law mirrors the EU database directive's cumulative-extraction rule.
