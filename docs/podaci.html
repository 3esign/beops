Vazduh — SEPA, zvanična mreža (satne srednje vrednosti, preliminarne)Air — SEPA, the official network (hourly means, preliminary)

Svaka tačka je stanica; boja je poslednja satna vrednost izabranog parametra. Tabela: poslednja vrednost, sat merenja po oznaci izvora, kretanje u prozoru. Vrednosti su preliminarne — tako ih izvor označava.Each dot is a station; the colour is the latest hourly value of the chosen parameter. Table: latest value, the source's own measurement hour, movement over the window. Values are preliminary — that is the source's own label.

Kroz vreme — kako se vrednosti slažu sat po satOver time — how the values stack up, hour by hour

Jedan stubić je jedan sat. Visina je najviša vrednost koju je bilo koja stanica objavila za taj sat, tanja crta unutar njega je gradski prosek tog sata. Prazan sat je sat bez ijednog prijema — ostaje prazan, ne postaje nula i ne povlači se linija preko njega. Traka ispod pokazuje koliko je stanica javilo u tom satu.One bar is one hour. Its height is the highest value any station published for that hour; the thin line inside it is the city mean for that hour. An empty hour is an hour with no reception at all — it stays empty, does not become a zero, and no line is drawn across it. The strip beneath shows how many stations reported in that hour.

Parking — prikazana slobodna mesta po garaži (vreme merenja nepoznato)Parking — displayed free spaces per garage (measurement time unknown)

Traka je poslednja vrednost, brojka desno; iskra je kretanje u prozoru.The operator publishes no measurement time: we know the value and when we received it, not how old it is. The bar is the latest value; the sparkline its movement over the window.
