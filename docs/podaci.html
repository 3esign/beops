Kroz vreme — kako se vrednosti slažu sat po satOver time — how the values stack up, hour by hour

Jedan stubić je jedan sat. Visina je najviša zabeležena vrednost; tanja crta je neponderisani prosek satnih proseka dostupnih nizova. To nije prosek celog grada. Prazan sat ostaje prazan. Traka ispod pokazuje koliko je nizova imalo vrednost u tom satu.One bar is one hour. Its height is the highest recorded value; the thin line is the unweighted mean of the available series' hourly means. It is not a city-wide mean. Empty hours stay empty. The strip counts series with a value that hour.
