Kako ovo radiHow this works

Jedan računar na univerzitetu, jedan zakazani zadatak na svakih pet minuta, i pravila koja se ne pregovaraju.One university computer, one scheduled task every five minutes, and rules that are not negotiable.

Dozvola pre kolektoraPermission before collector

Nijedan izvor se ne čita dok njegov robots.txt, zaglavlja i stranica licence ne budu sačuvani kao bajtovi sa hešom. Nepoznato nikada nije dozvola. Predstavljamo se pošteno kao Beops-Research-Collect/1.0 i kad izvor kaže ne — odgovor je ne, i to se zapisuje da niko ne pokuša ponovo.No source is read until its robots.txt, headers and licence page are stored as bytes with a hash. An unknown is never a permission. We identify honestly as Beops-Research-Collect/1.0, and when a source says no the answer is no — recorded, so nobody tries again next year.

Tri vremenaThree times

Izmereno, objavljeno, primljeno — nikad se ne stapaju. Neki izvori ne objavljuju vreme merenja uopšte: tada je vrednost tačna, a njena starost nepoznata, i tako se i crta.Measured, published, received — never collapsed into one. Some sources publish no measurement time at all: then the value is exact and its age is unknown, and it is drawn that way.

Nema izmišljenog merenjaNo invented measurementBroj koji nijedan izvor nije rekao ne postoji. Bez interpolacije, bez popunjavanja.A number no source reported does not exist. No interpolation, no back-filling.
Primljeno nije izmerenoReceived is not measuredVreme prijema je naše, ne gradsko.The reception time is ours, not the city's.
Prognoza nije merenjeForecast is not measurementProcena i prognoza imaju drugi potez i drugu boju — nikad boju merenja.Estimates and forecasts get a different stroke and never the colour of measurement.
Nedostaje nije nulaMissing is not zeroMrtav senzor, izdavač koji je odbio i nikad prikupljen izvor su tri različita zapisa.A dead sensor, a publisher that refused and a never-collected source are three different records.

Dokaz dozvole, i ono što je reklo neThe proof of permission, and what said no

Za svaki izvor čuvamo njegov sopstveni robots.txt kako je poslužen, zaglavlja tačnih adresa koje čitamo, stranicu licence i SHA-256 svakog od njih. Odbijanja se čuvaju sa klauzulom, pa se izvor ne otkriva ponovo za godinu dana i ne počne tiho da se sakuplja.For every source we store its own robots.txt as served, the headers of the exact URLs we read, the licence page, and a SHA-256 of each. Refusals are kept with their clause, so a source is not rediscovered next year and quietly collected.

Svaki put kad je ovaj sistem rekao nešto neistinitoEvery time this system said something untrue

Sistem koji krije sopstvene greške ne vredi ništa, jer jedino što treba da dokaže jeste da ne govori tiho neistine. Zato je ovaj spisak javan i dopisuje se, nikad se ne briše.A system that hides its own failures is worth nothing, because the one thing it must prove is that it does not quietly say untrue things. So this list is public, append-only, and never edited.
