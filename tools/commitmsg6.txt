Content into place, as a hypothesis that keeps its losers

Geoparsing is written up as a derived binding method, inferred_from_content,
and never a state and never a measurement. It is the only method in the table
where nobody asserted the location at all - not the publisher, not a register,
not a person - so it is a hypothesis wearing the shape of a fact, and a
coordinate is the most seductive kind of wrong answer this system can produce.

The derivation chain keeps every candidate considered, not only the winner,
because a wrong answer then shows whether the right place was never a candidate
- a gazetteer gap - or was a candidate and lost - a disambiguation failure. The
two need opposite fixes. It also records the margin over the runner-up, which
is a better confidence signal than any model's own score, since a near-tie
between two places named Zemun is exactly where a person must be asked.

Serbian specifics are written down rather than discovered later: two scripts in
one corpus, inflection where both words of a two-word name decline, settlement
names that are also common nouns, many settlements sharing a name, streets that
are personal names competing with person extraction, and Beograd being a city,
a district and a container of seventeen municipalities at once, so resolution
must return the level as well as the place. The gate is a gazetteer first,
rules before models, a hand-built held-out set, and precision, recall and
resolution accuracy reported separately because they fail differently.

The walk found building permits open by robots, which is the largest missing
layer of a changing city; the official gazette explicitly allowing its ELI
paths, which is at once the cleanest legal corpus and the best geoparser
material because gazette decisions name parcels and streets in words; and
data.europa.eu answering keyless with 8154 Serbia results, which is the
legitimate route to the CORDIS data that CORDIS itself disallows.

One defect fixed in place. A site serving its own HTML at /robots.txt with
HTTP 200 looks to both verdict engines exactly like a robots.txt with no rules,
so both answer allowed and the record says robots.txt served, which is false.
abs.gov.rs does this with 82 KB of analytics page. The tool now detects HTML
and records the path as returning HTML, treated as absent rather than as
permissive, and stores the body with a note at the top. Verified against the
live case.
